Rights & limits

Origin ownership & retention

Axel Urartu directs 100% Origin ownership/control of future original contributions: no voluntary assignment, license, or transfer to agents or others unless he chooses. Maintain provenance and contributor records.

This intended policy does not itself override pre-existing licenses, Copilot/platform terms, the repository's existing ISC declaration, or dependency/model/media rights; it does not bind contributors without an applicable agreement or prove exclusive title to every AI-assisted line. Original project contributions and licensed third-party components remain distinct. Changing licenses or repository visibility needs Axel's separate approval.

Your media, your responsibility

Upload only files you have permission to use. Check copyright, model/runtime licenses, privacy, likeness, and consent obligations. Uploading does not establish ownership or transfer anyone's rights. This is practical guidance, not legal advice.

Files stay on this device; AXES does not upload them to a service or send avatars to the language model. You control replacement and deletion. Deletion cannot erase backups or guarantee forensic erasure. Use your OS account and disk encryption for protection.

Available options

Use or edit 16 starter role templates, create profiles, enable/disable them, select an installed local model, create/delete conversations, and upload/replace/delete a role's avatar. Templates, enabled profiles, and active runs are different things.

JPEG/PNG images: 12 MiB, 2048 x 2048, single frame; normalized to PNG with metadata removed. H.264 MP4 videos: 25 MiB, 1920 x 1080, 15 seconds; require local FFmpeg/ffprobe, are re-encoded, and lose audio and metadata. Videos play only when you choose Play; no autoplay. No SVG, GIF, WebM, remote media, or generated images/videos.

Compute and privacy boundaries

One generation at a time; 120-second timeout; 64 KiB JSON requests; conservative 12,000-byte input and model context budgets; 1,024 output tokens. Long conversations may require a new conversation; AXES does not intentionally drop completed history to fit. Failed turns remain visible but are not replayed.

Node 24 LTS, app dependencies, Ollama, and a licensed model must be provisioned before disconnection. Hardware determines speed and supported model size. Cloud is disabled and unsupported here. No automatic downloads, tool execution, autonomous delegation, semantic memory, or OS image.

Pairing protects the local app from ordinary unpaired browser requests, not malware running as your user. The managed Ollama listener is a separate local process, not a secure sandbox. Runtime updaters and unrelated programs may still use the internet; strict air gaps require host-level controls.
